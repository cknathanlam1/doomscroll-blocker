# Doom­scroller *Blocker*

Earn your scroll.

Open YouTube and this app jumps in first. Do 10 push-ups in front of the camera and YouTube is yours for 15 minutes. Every screen in the flow below, from first launch to the Lock Screen timer. The Challenge screen is live: hit play to watch it count.

- **Shortcuts does the blocking**An automation brings this app forward when YouTube opens. There's no app picker inside the app.
- **Locked means locked, not closed**The app never closes YouTube or traps you. Leave anytime; YouTube just stays locked.
- **Readable from 3 ft away**During a set the phone sits on the floor. Giant numbers, high contrast, almost no words.
